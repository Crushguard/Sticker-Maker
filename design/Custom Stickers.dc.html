Love Stickers
PIP TECHNOLOGIES · ANDROID · V1.1

Custom Stickers · a 12-sticker pack with your two names, lettered on the phone

Right after Onboarding the user types their name and their love's name. The app letters 12 text-free Mango stickers with those names in the app's language, all 19 of them, RTL included, and hands the pack to WhatsApp. No AI runs in the app: the art is fixed, the lettering is a text layer drawn at runtime. It replaces the Customization step, which is gone from first run and from Settings; Home shows every theme.

01 · Brainstorm · the character

Who holds the sign?

The character is the sender. Whatever it does, the reader is the one being addressed, so a solo character makes every line land on the person receiving it.

PICK
Mango, text-free
Pro Already the app's most-added pack (96K adds) and the face of the listing; signs and hearts are its native props.
Con An orange cat on every surface risks blending into Clingy Mango.
The pink blob
Pro Genderless and culture-neutral; a blob is basically a sign with a face, so zones are huge.
Con No personality yet, and it would be the only blob in a library built on cats and photos.
A new puppy
Pro "Loyal" fits love; dogs test well with men, who are half the senders in a couple pack.
Con New art with no pack behind it; a second sheet pipeline to QA before launch.
Two-character couple
Pro Both names get a natural home; kiss and hug become literal scenes.
Con Fixes what the couple looks like for everyone, and two bodies push the lettering into corners.
Hands only
Pro Pure sign-holder: the biggest zones of any option and zero gender.
Con No face, no mood. Weakest as a gift, and the pack is a gift.
Why Mango, solo. The pack works as a message, not a portrait: Mango is the sender, so "Kiss me, Sara" is Mango asking, and it never has to guess the sender's gender or what the couple looks like. It carries the store listing and the top pack already, so the very first thing a new user makes is recognisably from this app. Twelve poses of one character also come off one art pipeline (two 3×2 sheets, generated, chroma-keyed) and the zones stay big because there is only one body in frame. The couple idea survives inside it: our_names is Mango holding a banner with both names.
02 · Brainstorm · the 12 stickers

Poses, zones and phrases

Started from the given slots and made one swap: marry_me → call_me. Two of the six relations (Wife, Husband) are already married, and a first-run pack should be all daily drivers; "Call me, Sara" gets sent every week. The gift tag on for_you is the tightest zone, so its phrase is the shortest. Every pose has an obvious empty area: a held sign, a heart, a banner, a bubble, or open space kept clear by scaling the art down and anchoring it.

The wow moment
A · Stamping grid
Pro Stickers slam into a 3×4 grid one by one; very legible, each name is read 12 times.
Con 12 × 250 ms is three seconds of waiting for the last one, and it has nothing to show while lettering is still running.
B · One character waits, then the pack popsPICK
Pro The chosen character waits on screen (Mango taps its foot and checks its watch) over a slim progress bar bound to the 12 renders; then the 12 stickers pop in from the centre outward. One animation, and it is the character the user just picked, so the wait itself is on-brand.
Con Needs one waiting loop per character (4 clips); the bar must be honest and track real progress.
C · Arriving as chat bubbles
Pro Shows the payoff in context: "Good morning, Sara" lands in a fake chat.
Con A fake WhatsApp inside the app, and 12 bubbles need a scroll; the pack never appears as a pack.

05 · For the developer · lettering spec

How the app writes on the stickers

Fonts · one pairing per script, all Google Fonts, SIL OFL
Sara Aymen ¡Hola!Baloo 2 · 800
Latin, Latin-ext, Devanagari
सारा · अयमनBaloo 2 · 800
hi, mr
سارة · أيمن · پښتوBaloo Bhaijaan 2 · 800
ar, fa, ur, ps (+ Latin)
Саша · שרהRubik · 800
ru, uk, he
Pick the font from the string, not the UI language: any Arabic-range character → Baloo Bhaijaan 2; Hebrew or Cyrillic → Rubik; else Baloo 2. That is what makes "Love you, سارة" in an English UI work. Bundle the three families (static 800 weights, ~600 KB). Verify Pashto letters (ښ ځ څ ډ ړ ږ ګ ڼ) render in Baloo Bhaijaan 2 on the target OS; if any are missing, fall back per glyph to Noto Sans Arabic ExtraBold.
Fit algorithm · 512-px space
Size start at min(zone.h × 0.82, 128) px, step down 2 px to a 20 px floor. Line height 1.08 × size. Tracking −1%.
Lines one line if it fits zone.w; else the best two-line split on spaces (the split with the narrowest longer line) if 2 × line height ≤ zone.h. Never three. Names never break inside.
Floor if 20 px still overflows, keep 20 px and clip nothing: show the name-step note "That's the limit" (the input already caps at 14 characters, so this only happens for wide scripts). Sizes 20 px ≈ 5 px at tray scale, so the floor is legibility, not layout.
Placement centre the block on (zone.cx, zone.cy), rotate by zone.rotate around that point, align centre. Direction from the string: RTL if it contains Arabic or Hebrew characters. Bidi is handled by the text engine; do not reorder anything yourself.
Colour per sticker from ink: brown #3B2114 on cream signs and white bubbles, white on the red heart, rose #C23359 on white banners and in open space. Open-space zones add a 5 px white stroke (paint order stroke, then fill).
Heart in our_names the ❤ token is a vector heart (the app mark path), 0.78 × font size, baseline −12%, same colour as the ink, with 0.08 em gaps. Never a font glyph, never an emoji.
Then composite art + lettering, draw the die-cut white outline (8 px at 512, round joins) around the union, export 512 × 512 WebP ≤ 100 KB, quality 0.84. Cache per (slot, tone, lang, names).

08 · Animated · yes, lettering still works

Higgsfield motion under a runtime text layer

The lettering is a layer the app draws on top, so it does not care whether the art below is one frame or thirty. The only rule: the lettering surface must not move. Props (sign, heart, banner, bubble) stay still and only the cat moves; open-space zones (good night, kiss, hug) just need the cat to stay inside its own area. Both clips below were made with the library pipeline: still → Kling 3.0 Turbo, 3 s, 1:1 → chroma key → 10 fps animated WebP with a 3-frame crossfade. The sign in the right clip drifts by 1.8% between first and last frame, which is compression noise, not motion.

To animate all 12
Prompt rule "static camera, flat green unchanged, the [sign/heart/banner/bubble] stays perfectly still and blank, only the cat moves, small 2D motion, no text". Re-roll any clip whose zone diff exceeds 5%.
Zones re-measure per clip from frame 0 (the still is the first frame). A live entry per sticker in mango.json: file, zone, and a stable flag from the check.
Budget 12 clips at 4.5 credits each; WhatsApp caps animated stickers at 500 KB and the pack must be all-animated, so the 12 ship as "Aymen ❤ Sara · Live" next to the static pack, same as the library's Live layer.
Lettering unchanged: same fonts, same fit, same colours, drawn once and stamped on every frame; the die-cut outline is computed on the union of all frames so it does not flicker.
09 · Brainstorm · who says it

Four characters, same 12 poses, same phrases

All four come from packs already in the library, so the choice reads as "which of our stickers do you like" rather than a new decision to learn. Poses, slots, phrases, tones and the fit algorithm are shared; only the art and its zone file differ (one JSON per character, measured on that character). A fifth character is one more sheet pair plus a JSON.

A · A dedicated screen before the names
Pro Big art, room for a name and a one-line personality per character.
Con One more screen in a flow that promised to be short; the user decides before seeing a single lettered sticker.
B · A row of four tiles on the first stepPICK
Pro No extra screen. "Who says it?" sits above "What's your name?", Mango preselected, and the live preview swaps character the moment you tap. The same row sits above the reveal grid so the whole pack can be re-cast after seeing it, for free (lettering is runtime).
Con Tiles are 46 px, so the characters must be recognisable as silhouettes; the hug pose is used because it reads at any size.
C · Only on the reveal
Pro Zero questions before the wow moment.
Con The building screen has to show a default character, and someone who dislikes cats has already sat through Mango waiting for them.

Measurements, motion, rules

Screens · 390 × 844 reference
Top strip 40 px: language chip (36 px pill, globe 15, native name, 1 px #E7EAEF) at the start edge; ghost Skip at the end edge. Two-segment progress bar 4 px, rose for done, 6 px gap.
Name field 52 px, radius 12, 1 px rose border while active (amber #E0B778 on error), user icon 17, counter "n/14" in 11 px mono (amber at 14). Note line 12.5 px under the field: limit, emoji error, or missing-name error.
Relation chips 36 px pills, wrap, single-select: Girlfriend · Boyfriend · Wife · Husband · Crush · Partner · Mom · Friend. Default Girlfriend. Mom and Friend hide the Flirty tone and use the family phrase overrides (kiss, hug, be_mine). Character tiles four equal tiles under the title on the first step (46 px art, label 11/600, rose tint + border when selected) and a 40 px version above the reveal grid.
Preview 210 px sticker, centred in the free space, white die-cut via drop-shadow. Primary 52/14: "Continue" on the name step, "Make our stickers" on the love step (disabled until a valid name).
Building character loop 224 px with the die-cut outline, bar 200 × 4 (#E7EAEF track, rose fill), title 22/800, sub 14. Reveal header 52 with the 28 px tray heart + pack name; meta 12 mono + Sweet/Flirty chips 32 px; 3-column grid, 110 px tiles, 16 radius, #F6F7F9; pinned Add bar 52/14 with the library's five states; ghost "Not now" 44 px.
RTL set dir=rtl on the root for ar, fa, ur, ps, he. Everything mirrors; progress bar fills from the right; the chip moves to the right edge, Skip to the left.
Motion · ms
Preview re-letter: on every keystroke, no debounce; the lettering layer crossfades 120.
Language switch: sheet 320 ease-out up; app re-renders immediately; preview re-letters with the new phrase set.
Building: character loop 3 s, 10 fps, plays from frame 0; bar 0→100% ≥ 1700, linear, bound to real progress (12 renders); 420 hold at 100%; cut to reveal.
Reveal: header and meta fade 300; tiles scale .2→1, 420, cubic-bezier(.2,.9,.3,1.3), delay = 95 × distance-from-centre in tile units (0, 95, 135, 170…); Add bar fades 300 after the last tile.
Tone switch: lettering crossfade 160, tiles static. Add → WhatsApp: existing Add flow (download is instant here: files are local), then WhatsApp's own confirm; on return Home slides in, toast "Added to WhatsApp" 2400.
Reduced motion: no pulse or burst; heart fades to the grid over 300.
Rules · edge cases
Names: max 14 characters (grapheme clusters), trimmed, internal spaces collapsed, no emoji or pictographic symbols (validation note, button disabled). Letters, marks, digits, apostrophes, hyphens and dots are fine.
Skip on the name step → your name is empty → pack "For Sara", our_names letters only the love name on the banner. Skip on the love step → Home, nothing built; the entry point stays available later from Create › "Name stickers" (not designed here).
Phrases fall back to English if a language lacks a key; the name is inserted verbatim, never transliterated or re-cased. Initials use the first grapheme; scripts without case stay as typed.
The 12 renders happen on a background thread; the heart shows real progress. If WhatsApp is missing, the existing "WhatsApp isn't installed" sheet shows on Add and the pack is still saved under My Packs › Made by you.
Add, never replace: Customization and its Edit-themes path are untouched; first run simply routes Onboarding → this flow → Home with all themes on.
