05

Add flow · five states

The same control moves through idle → downloading → sent to WhatsApp → added. Progress fills the bar itself; nothing else on the screen changes. The card pill on Home follows the same states in miniature. Tap Add in any frame to run it live.

1 · Idle
Primary bar, filled rose, WhatsApp-style glyph 19 + "Add to WhatsApp". The only filled primary on the screen.
2 · Downloading
Bar turns to rose tint; a rose fill grows left to right with the percentage in the label. Steady, no fake acceleration.
3 · Sent to WhatsApp
Spinner + "Sent to WhatsApp…" while WhatsApp shows its own confirmation (simulated as a neutral bottom sheet). Cancel returns to idle.
4 · Added
Bar flips to white with a green check and "Added to WhatsApp"; dark toast confirms. The pack now lists under My Packs.
Retry · download failed
Attention amber outline, "Download failed · Retry". Fill stays where it stopped. Tap retries from zero.
Card pill · same states, small
36px pill: Add → 64% → Sent… → Added (green). Tap Add on any card here to watch it run.
06

Create · three steps

The raised Create button opens Import. Photos, camera or video clips (animated pack), 3 to 30 stickers. Tapping Next runs an on-device subject cut-out on every pick, so most users only review. Fix edges with Brush and Erase, add a word, keep the white outline WhatsApp recommends, then name the pack and Add. Export is 512×512 WebP under 100 KB (500 KB animated). Nothing is uploaded.

Import
Segmented Photos / Camera / Video · 3-col picker with rose check squares · footer primary reads the count, disabled under 3 · helper line says what Next does.
Cut out · auto result
Dark tool bar (PDF Reader markup pattern): Auto · Brush · Erase · Text · Zoom · transparency checker behind the cut subject · white-outline toggle · undo in the header · rail with a green check per finished sticker.
Cut out · fixing edges

My Packs · Saved

My Packs lists what is in WhatsApp and what you made. The heart in its toolbar, with a count, opens Saved: every pack you hearted on Home or a pack page. The ⋯ menu re-adds or removes the app's local copy; WhatsApp keeps its own. Hearted packs are no longer a tab; they sit under the ♥ Saved chip on Home.

Settings · rating · confirmations

Mapped from RecoverMe. Settings opens from the gear on Home and My Packs: one hero card for the setting that matters (New pack alerts), grouped rows under mono eyebrows with values on the right, an arrow for anything that leaves the app, and the free-forever card last. Rating is RecoverMe's sheet: tap a star and it moves on by itself. Five stars asks for a Google Play review; anything less opens a private feedback box. Confirmations are bottom sheets with two equal buttons; destructive ones are filled red and say what stays in WhatsApp.
